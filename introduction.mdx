Woodstock is a **mobile-first, cloud-first agentic team workspace**. Instead of filing things yourself, you drop them into a channel and a background agent does the organizing—then pings you when it's done.

## The core loop

Everything in Woodstock follows one simple pattern:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop something into a channel">
    An idea, a voice memo from a meeting, an accounting document, or a quick note to yourself—anything goes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An agent organizes it">
    A background agent transcribes, extracts, files, and links the item into your workspace's shared knowledge graph.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get pinged proactively">
    When the work is done—or when the agent needs a decision—it reaches out to you, so nothing falls through the cracks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
